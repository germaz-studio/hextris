Hextris is an addictive, fast-paced puzzle game inspired by Tetris, ported natively to Android for F-Droid by Oskar Gerlicz-Kowalczuk (Germaz Studio).

Rotate the outer hexagon to catch incoming colored blocks. Match three or more blocks of the same color to clear them before they stack beyond the gray hexagon boundary!

Features:
* Fullscreen responsive layout with high-refresh rate support
* Simple and responsive touch controls (tap left/right to rotate)
* Local high-score tracking and streak combo multipliers
* Zero analytics, zero ads, zero telemetry
* 100% Free and Open Source (GNU GPL v3.0)
* Completely offline, zero permissions required
